`TLS-RENEW`, `TLS-REVOKE`, `TLS-VERIFY`, `TLS-POLICY-READ`,

## DNS

`DNS-ZONE-DELETE-REQUEST`, `DNS-ZONE-RECONCILE`, `DNS-RECORD-LIST`,
`DNS-RECORD-CREATE`, `DNS-RECORD-UPDATE`, `DNS-RECORD-DELETE`,
`DNS-OBSERVATION-READ`.

`DNSSEC-STATUS-READ`, `DNSSEC-ENABLE`, `DNSSEC-DISABLE`,
`DNSSEC-ROLLOVER-BEGIN`, `DNSSEC-ROLLOVER-CONTINUE`, `DNSSEC-VERIFY`.

material through UI/API/CLI.
