Privacy principles

The final WCP360 website should collect the minimum information required to deliver an explicitly requested service. Product telemetry, community identity, support diagnostics and commercial records must use separate purposes and retention rules.

Website prototype

The static prototype contains no analytics SDK, account system, advertising tracker or form submission backend. Browser and hosting-provider logs may exist only when the site is later deployed by an operator.

Customer servers

A WCP360 installation is controlled by its server owner. Hosted domains, files, mail, database content and credentials are not required by the vendor licensing service. Optional diagnostics require separate, revocable consent.

Purpose separation

  • Licensing verifies signed entitlements and minimal installation identity.
  • Marketplace clients retrieve signed catalogue and package metadata.
  • Support diagnostics are opt-in, scoped and sanitized.
  • Community profiles and posts remain separate from customer hosting data.

Terms principles

WCP360 is under active design. Prototype screens and commands are provided for evaluation and do not constitute a production service, warranty or service-level commitment.

  • Do not execute reserved .example installer URLs or treat them as live release endpoints.
  • Do not rely on prototype availability, pricing, compatibility or roadmap content as a contractual promise.
  • Security controls described here are target properties until verified in a released implementation.
  • Final terms will distinguish open-source software, commercial services, marketplace packages and third-party publishers.

GDPR & data governance

The product architecture is intended to support data inventories, residence constraints, retention, legal holds, export and reviewed erasure or anonymization. These tools assist operators; they do not automatically make an operator compliant.

Data minimizationCollect only what a declared control-plane or service purpose requires.
ResidencyApply placement policy to accounts, databases and backups with verifiable adapter receipts.
RetentionResolve policy across live data, logs, backups, legal holds and immutable evidence.
ErasurePlan, approve and record deletion or anonymization without silently violating recovery or legal duties.
PortabilityExport customer-controlled data through stable formats and documented workflows.

Software and content licenses

The intended public WCP360 core is MIT-licensed. Individual third-party dependencies, applications and marketplace extensions may use different licenses and must publish their own notices and compatibility.

Final release archives will include machine-readable SBOM data and applicable license notices. Brand assets, documentation and website content require a separately published content policy if they are not covered by the software license.
